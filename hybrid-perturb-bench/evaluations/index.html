Milestones 1 and 2 under one contract — combinations, held-out singles, and the metric files.

Evaluation — Norman 2019 Perturb-seq

Version labels: Milestone 1 (baselines) and Milestone 2 (hybrid), same contract. Norman et al. 2019 Perturb-seq (CRISPRa, K562), as harmonized by scPerturb: 111,445 cells × 33,694 genes, 11,855 control cells. Profiles are pseudobulk counts per perturbation, library-normalized to 1e4 + log1p. The split holds out entire perturbations: 84 train singles, 21 held-out test singles (seed 0); the 131 two-gene combinations were never used in any training and form the compositional-generalization test. The contract scores predicted against real pseudobulk profiles on the top 2000 eval genes: Pearson Δ (pearson_delta), MSE Δ, and DEG overlap @50 — the share of the top-50 |Δ| genes recovered (deg_overlap_at_k in the files, k = 50). The ceiling is a split-half of real data, Spearman-Brown corrected.

The hybrid is below the baselines on singles, and that stays published. Hybrid Pearson Δ 0.5366 sits below the global mean 0.5876 and the ridge 0.5785 (ceiling 0.9167); DEG @50 tells the same story — hybrid 0.3143 against global mean 0.3314 and ridge 0.3326 (ceiling 0.6590). No hyperparameter was adjusted after seeing this result. GEARS was attempted as a comparator and not completed — no GEARS predictions exist and no GEARS numbers are reported.
